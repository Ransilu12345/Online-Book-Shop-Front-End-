Featured Book: Ghosts Of Honolulu

Author: Mark Harmon and Leon Carroll Jr.

Published: 2023

Genre: Non-Fiction

Description: This book uncovers the untold story of Douglas Wada, a Japanese American spy for the U.S. Navy during World War II. It details his role in investigating the Pearl Harbor attack and his mission to counteract Japanese espionage in Hawaii. The narrative highlights Wada's challenges as he navigated the complexities of his heritage while working to protect both his country and his community.
