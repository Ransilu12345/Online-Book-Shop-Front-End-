Featured Book: Counting Miracles

Author: Nicholas Sparks

Published: 2024

Genre: Inspirational Fiction

Description: In "Counting Miracles," Tanner Hughes, an Army Ranger, embarks on a journey to find his estranged father after the death of his grandmother. In the small town of Asheboro, North Carolina, he meets Kaitlyn Cooper, a doctor and single mother, who has her own challenges. As their lives intertwine, they encounter an elderly man, Jasper, who is determined to protect a legendary white deer from poachers. The story explores themes of belonging, family, and unexpected miracles.
