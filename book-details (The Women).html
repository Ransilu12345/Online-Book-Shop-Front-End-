Featured Book: The Women

Author: Kristin Hannah

Published: 2013

Genre: Historical Fiction

Description: Set during the Vietnam War, The Women follows Frankie, a dedicated nurse who faces the chaos and trauma of war. As she navigates her experiences in a male-dominated field, she grapples with her own emotional scars and the complexities of love and loss. The novel explores themes of female friendship, resilience, and the lasting impacts of war on women's lives.
